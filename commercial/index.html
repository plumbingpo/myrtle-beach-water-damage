Risk Assessment

Risk Assessments That Help Prevent Commercial Water Damage

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe conditions on their properties. Injuries, lawsuits, and damaged property can all trace back to a sudden failure from inadequate waterproofing, concealed moisture, or plumbing past its prime. Using thermal imaging and moisture monitoring equipment, Myrtle Beach Restoration performs professional risk assessments that check how vulnerable each area is to water getting in. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

An assessment does two jobs: finding vulnerabilities protects people and the property, and the record of due diligence can protect you legally should something happen. Courts and insurance companies look favorably on property owners who maintain regular professional inspections and act on expert recommendations. We recommend annual moisture assessments for all commercial properties, with more frequent inspections for high-risk areas such as basements, mechanical rooms, and aging plumbing systems.
