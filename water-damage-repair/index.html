Signs You Need Water Damage Repair in Myrtle Beach

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • A door frame or slab swelled, so the door rubs or fails to close.
  • Hardwood boards have cupped, crowned or lifted and did not flatten after drying.
  • Baseboards and door casings are warped or no longer sit flat against the wall.
  • Drywall was cut out during drying and the wall cavities are still open.
  • The edges of your laminate or vinyl planks have puffed up, curled or pulled apart.
  • A leak overhead left the ceiling drywall cracked, sagging or falling apart.
  • Walking over the formerly wet area, you feel soft spots in the floor.

Why Water Damage Repair Is Not a DIY Job

Repair after water damage is not ordinary remodeling. The person rebuilding needs to know what the water did, where it went and whether the structure behind the wall is truly dry. Our team works from the moisture records taken during drying, so we know which studs were wet, which subfloor was treated and where to look twice. That history keeps problems from being covered over with new drywall and fresh paint.

Matching is the other skill. A patch that shows is a repair you notice every day. Our finishers blend the texture on walls, carry paint to a natural stopping point and, where it exists, find flooring that aligns with your current boards. If nothing matches, you get options from us, for example new flooring run to a doorway. We also handle permits where the work requires them and keep your insurer updated.

Water Damage Repair Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Hold off on heavy traffic and wet cleaning until new adhesives, caulk and paint have cured.
  • Future repairs match better when you have held on to extra flooring, tile and the paint information.
  • Rebuilt bathroom walls last longer when the exhaust fan runs through and after each shower.
  • In case the insurance company asks down the road, photograph anything damaged while it is still in place.
  • Once a year, renew the caulk at sinks, showers and tubs to keep water from entering the wall.
  • Do not paint or patch over a water stain until the source is fixed.
