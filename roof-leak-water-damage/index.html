Roof Leak Water Damage Help in Myrtle Beach

Where you see the stain and where the rain gets in are usually two different places. Water enters at a raised shingle, a split in the flashing or a worn skylight seal, follows the rafters and decking downhill, and finally lands on the insulation. By the time a brown ring shows on the ceiling, the attic above it has often been wet for a while. We find the full wet area and dry it out.

To be clear, roof repair is not what we do, water damage is. Inside the home, our team takes out saturated insulation, dries the wood and drywall, treats any mold and repairs the ceiling. When the roof is still letting water through, a temporary tarp is something we can install, and we will coordinate with your roofer so the repair comes before the ceiling is closed.

Right Now

What to Do Right Now

  1. Shut Off Wet CircuitsIf water is dripping through a light fixture, a ceiling fan or near an outlet, switch off that circuit at the breaker and leave the fixture alone.
  2. Catch the DripsPut a pan or bucket beneath each drip, and shift rugs, electronics and furniture clear of the wet area. Protect the floor with plastic sheeting or old towels.
  3. Relieve a Ceiling BulgeA ceiling that sags with trapped water can be drained: stay off to the side and make a small opening at its lowest spot, with a bucket underneath.
  4. Stay Off the RoofA wet roof and an unlit attic are both places to stay out of. Note from inside where the water is coming in and leave the rest to professionals.
  5. Photograph the DamageBefore anything is moved or discarded, get video and photos of the attic, the ceiling and damaged items, and then phone us so drying can begin.

Why It Happens

What Causes It

  • Corroded or loose metal flashing where the roof meets a chimney, a vent or a wall.
  • Clogged gutters that back water up under the shingles at the roof edge.
  • A valley liner that has worn through at the point where the most runoff passes.
  • Wind-torn, curling or split shingles that leave the underlayment open to rain.
  • Aging, cracked seals around a skylight frame that no longer keep water out.
What Is at Risk

What the Water Damages

Ceiling Drywall

A ceiling panel left damp will discolor, bow and weaken. Seams split, the tape lets go, and a heavy enough section can drop with little warning.

Rafters and Decking

Rafters, trusses and decking that get wet again and again begin to darken, swell and soften. Decay here means less strength in what carries the roof.

Mold in the Attic

Damp framing, paper-faced drywall, warmth and darkness give mold what it needs. It may take hold in 24 to 48 hours and work its way over the underside of the sheathing.

Attic Insulation

Once soaked, insulation flattens, loses its value and keeps the drywall under it damp for days. Fiberglass can sometimes be dried. Removal is the usual answer for wet cellulose.

Insurance

What About Insurance?

Homeowners policies often cover interior water damage when a roof leak is sudden and accidental, such as wind tearing off shingles or a limb puncturing the roof. A leak put down to an old, worn or neglected roof is usually denied, and fixing the roof may be treated separately from the interior work. We document the damage with photos and moisture readings for your adjuster, but coverage depends on your policy, so read it or ask your agent.

What It Costs

What Drives the Price

  • How long the roof leaked before it was found, and any mold growth.
  • Whether wet attic insulation can be dried in place, and how large an area it covers.
  • Drywall removal: how much of the ceiling and walls is beyond saving.
  • How far down the leak traveled, from the attic to the walls and floor.
