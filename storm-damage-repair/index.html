How to Prevent Storm Damage Repair Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Branches break in high wind, so keep nearby trees pruned away from the roof and siding
  • Runoff should flow away from your foundation, so fix low spots and add downspout extensions
  • Clean out downspouts and gutters regularly, which lets heavy rainfall drain away as it should
  • Learn where the main water shutoff is and how to close it if pipes are damaged in a storm
  • Good attic insulation and ventilation stop ice dams from forming along the roof edge
  • Schedule a roof inspection every year so weak spots are repaired before storm season

How to Tell You Need Storm Damage Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Swollen ceiling panels or blistered, peeling paint in the days after a storm
  • Missing, damaged or lifted shingles you can see from the ground
  • A basement or ground floor that floods when the drains cannot keep up
  • Water getting past doors, windows and wall penetrations when wind pushes the rain
  • Sagging or detached gutters that spill rainwater down the siding
  • Metal flashing pulled away from vents, valleys or the edge of the roof
  • A musty smell indoors in the days after heavy rain
